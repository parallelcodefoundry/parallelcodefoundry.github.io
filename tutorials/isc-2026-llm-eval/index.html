What This Covers

The tutorial follows the slide deck section by section: first building shared vocabulary, then covering common failure modes and the practices needed for reproducible evaluation.

1

Introduction

Tutorial goals: how to evaluate LLMs in HPC and scientific research, and what can go wrong when they are used as research components.

2

Introduction to LLMs

Shared vocabulary for tokens, text generation, models, and the sources of randomness in LLM outputs.

3

How to Lie with LLM Results

Pitfalls such as ignoring randomness, cherry picking, benchmark leakage, rubric drift, hidden human intervention, and unreported cost.

4

Experimental Design for Stochastic Components

How to design experiments when LLM calls, agents, or other parts of the workflow can vary from run to run.

5

Transparency and Reproducibility

Logging generations, saving agent traces, and making enough raw output available for review and replay.

6

Human Validation: When and How

Where human scoring helps, why it is hard to reproduce, and how to pair it with less subjective metrics.

7

Baselines, Metrics, and Ablations

Representative baselines, probabilistic metrics, ablations, and performance measurement for LLM-generated code.
