Towards Integrating LLMs in HPC Software Development

SC '24 BoF

Tuesday, November 19th 2024, 5:15pm - 6:45pm EST

This page is currently under construction. Please check back later for updates.

The inaugural BoF will take place at SC '24 on Tuesday at 5:15 - 6:45pm EST in room B310.

The Towards Integrating LLMs in HPC Software Development BoF will bring together researchers, developers, and users of large language models (LLMs) in the HPC community to discuss the challenges and opportunities of integrating LLMs in HPC software development. The BoF will feature a panel discussion with experts from academia, industry, and national laboratories, followed by a Q&A session with the audience.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Correctness and trustworthiness of LLMs for scientific code
  • Performance and scalability of LLM generated code
  • Legal and ethical considerations
  • LLMs for Research Software Engineering
  • LLMs for HPC education and training
  • Integrating LLMs into existing HPC tools

BoF Organizers:

  • Abhinav Bhatele, University of Maryland
  • William Godoy, Oak Ridge National Laboratory
  • Arjun Guha, Northeastern University
  • Harshitha Menon, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory
  • Konstantinos Parasyris, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory
  • Pedro Valero-Lara, Oak Ridge National Laboratory
  • Daniel Nichols, University of Maryland
